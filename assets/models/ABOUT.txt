Meaning models used by the keyboard, run on the phone with ONNX Runtime (MIT licence,
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime).

e5.onnx
  multilingual-e5-small by Liang Wang et al., Microsoft (intfloat)
  https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small
  Licence: MIT

It is the published 8-bit version with the word-piece table cut down to the pieces
English and Vietnamese need (vocab.txt, from the model's XLM-RoBERTa piece list).
poses_e5.bin holds the model's readings of short example phrases for each pose
(tools/examples.py and tools/reteach.py make it).
MiniLM (paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2) shipped alongside until Nov 2026.
Nothing else was changed.
