Meaning models used by the keyboard, run on the phone with ONNX Runtime (MIT licence,
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime).

minilm.onnx
  paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 by sentence-transformers
  https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2
  Licence: Apache License 2.0

e5.onnx
  multilingual-e5-small by Liang Wang et al., Microsoft (intfloat)
  https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small
  Licence: MIT

Both are the published 8-bit versions with the word-piece table cut down to the pieces
English and Vietnamese need (vocab.txt, from the models' shared XLM-RoBERTa piece list).
poses_*.bin hold the models' readings of short example phrases for each pose.
Nothing else was changed.
